How it works

Five things, no settings safari.

01 · Onboarding

Five minutes to a plan that starts where you are.

Tell the coach what race you're training for, when it is, your experience level, and your weekly availability. Connect Apple Health. The plan starts from your actual fitness — not a textbook athlete's.

02 · Daily view

Today's session, ready to go.

Type, duration, target zone, coaching notes. Completed workouts pull in from your watch automatically and match against the plan. No logging. No spreadsheets.

03 · Morning Check-In

A short conversation before you lace up.

Informed by last night's sleep and today's plan. Reply if you're exhausted, sore, crushing it, or anywhere between. The coach adjusts before you head out the door.

04 · Plan Negotiation

The core idea: nothing changes without your sign-off.

Tell the coach what's going on. It proposes a revised plan — shifted long runs, reduced volume, rearranged key workouts. You see a visual diff card and tap Accept, Modify, or Reject. No surprise rewrites.

05 · Race week

Course-specific intelligence as the race approaches.

Altitude delta vs. your training elevation, expected weather, pacing tuned to the terrain, phase-appropriate prep — heat acclimation, travel timing, taper intensity.

Optional · Performance Thresholds

Know your FTP, threshold run pace, or swim CSS? Mention them in chat once. Pacing targets become specific numbers (8:15/mi on the long run) instead of effort descriptors. No settings screen — the coach just remembers.
